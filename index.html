Chapter 1 • Forensic Computational Biology

The Vendor Catastrophe & The Double rRNA Confounder

When the sequencing provider delivered their initial analysis, the results were completely unusable. Here is how forensic sequence inspection overturned vendor errors and identified the true composition of the libraries.

⚠️ The Vendor Error: Wrong Reference Organism

The commercial provider aligned all 16 libraries to Saccharomyces cerevisiae (baker's yeast). Gene IDs in the delivered matrix were SGD systematic names (YDL151C, YER174C, Q0045/COX1); the 7,127 features matched SGD R64 exactly, and uniquely mapped reads were only 453–2,284 per sample (0.006–0.03% of input). Delivered PCA, differential expression, and human-only MSigDB Hallmark gene set analyses were completely void.

✓ Forensic Recovery: True Biological Identity

Direct BLAST of the most abundant sequence read returned 100% identity to Pleurotus 28S ribosomal RNA. The reads themselves were intact; the NovaSeq X Plus 94 bp single-end libraries with 14 nt directional UMIs were high quality, but required an adapted pipeline grounded in the NASA GeneLab GL-DPPD-7101-G consensus specification.

Hidden Giant 1: Nuclear rDNA Array

Nuclear ribosomal DNA accounts for 19.9% to 69.7% of reads (mean 53.5%) across the libraries. Standard reference annotations collapse or omit these repetitive arrays, scattering reads into multi-mapping noise.

Hidden Giant 2: Unannotated Mitochondrial rRNA

Of the reads surviving nuclear depletion, ~60.9% mapped to a single mitochondrial locus (CM148777.1:52,604–56,069). This locus was completely unannotated in all public P. ostreatus GTF files, causing standard biotype-filtering pipelines to silently discard it as unassigned noise.

Why BOM_ss5 Won

The cultivar is P. ostreatus cv. "Harbor Blue P01", a commercial blue oyster mushroom. BOM_ss5 is a single-spore isolate matching the cultivar. Crucially, BOM_ss5 uniquely resolves the tandem nuclear rDNA repeat array (JBQVBD010000012.1:2,298,112-2,346,320) that other assemblies collapse, assigning +6.9% more mRNA counts across every single sample.

3' UTR Extension Diagnostic

A prevailing hypothesis was that truncated annotated 3' UTRs caused high unassigned read rates in 3'-tag data. Extending 97.7% of gene models by up to 500 bp (neighbour-capped) increased read assignment from 15.5% to 15.6% (+0.1%). This rigorous diagnostic proved truncated UTRs were negligible; unannotated rRNA was the true culprit.

Chapter 4 • Tissue Differentiation & Specialization

Biological Signal: What Survives the Crucible?

Tissue specificity index τ computed on group means can easily manufacture false markers from single-replicate outliers. We applied a stringent cross-replicate robustness test: a marker is accepted only if every replicate of its tissue exceeds the maximum in all other tissues.

Nodule: Early Primordium Specialization

22 / 50 Robust Markers

Nodules represent early fruiting-body primordia. Their signature is defined by protective and structural proteins:

  • Cerato-platanin (CP1): Eliciting plant response-like proteins (AAD021_000251, τ = 0.984).
  • Small Heat Shock Proteins: HSP16 (AAD021_005235, τ = 0.978) and 17.8 kDa Class I HSP.
  • Class I Hydrophobin 7: Aerial hyphal coat and fruiting initiation (AAD021_002063, τ = 0.941).
  • Brown Co-expression Module: 320 genes, r = +0.85, FDR = 0.0155 (oxidoreductase activity).

Exudophore: Oxidative & Secretory Engine

37 / 50 Robust Markers

The exudophore produces liquid guttation droplets. It exhibits a coordinated extracellular H2O2-generating and lignocellulose-degrading signature:

  • Alcohol Oxidase 1 (AOX1): Robust extracellular H2O2 generator (AAD021_011248, τ = 0.954).
  • Acetate--CoA Ligase CCL3: Central carbon activator (AAD021_002509, τ = 0.967).
  • Formate & Aldehyde Dehydrogenases: C1 detoxification and oxidation machinery (AAD021_003933).
  • AA9 LPMO: Lytic polysaccharide monooxygenase for white-rot breakdown (AAD021_003985).
Honest Science: Fuzzy and Exuding Mycelium Fail Robustness Tests

In initial mean-based analyses, Fuzzy Mycelium appeared to express ostreolysin A6 and dye-decolorizing peroxidase (DyP). However, cross-replicate auditing proved these signals were driven by a single outlier replicate (1F). Only 1 of 50 markers survived replicate testing (2%), and zero co-expression modules survived FDR correction. They are vegetative mycelium with no defensible differential signature in this dataset.

Targeted Backward MILP Gapfilling

A global MILP gapfill across the full ~31,000 ModelSEED reaction pool stalled indefinitely (>10.5 hours). By implementing a targeted backward graph walk (depth 6) from each unproducible target, candidate reaction pools were restricted to 1.8k–8k reactions. All three essential gaps (NAD: rxn07095, FAD: rxn00122, UDP-GlcNAc: rxn28025) solved cleanly in 2.5–9.3 seconds.

Biotin Auxotrophy Discovery

Biotin remained unreachable despite targeted gapfilling. Biological verification confirmed that many basidiomycetes are natural biotin auxotrophs. Because the mycoponic nutrient medium (MNM v3) contains malt extract and peptone supplying B-vitamins, biotin was properly added to the medium definition rather than forcing an artificial de novo synthetic route.

Chapter 6 • Mitochondrial Transcriptomics & Evolution

Mitochondrial Genome Architecture & The 2,135-Fold Gain

RNA-seq recovers a complete 73.2 kb mitochondrial genome that its own assembly failed to annotate, illuminating intron homing endonuclease dynamics, mitochondrial expression, and nuclear-mitochondrial retrograde signaling.

The 2,135-Fold Annotation Gain

Public deposited annotations of the P. ostreatus mitochondrial contig (CM148777.1) contained almost zero functional features. By combining RNA-seq read coverage clustering with tRNAscan-SE, Aragon, and protein homology, our corrected annotation recovered:

  • 13 Core Respiratory Subunits: nad1–nad6, cox1–cox3, cob, atp6, atp8, atp9.
  • 25 Functional tRNAs covering all 20 amino acids.
  • Large (rrnL) and Small (rrnS) Subunit rRNAs, identifying the missing read sink.
  • Group I / Group II Intron Catalogue: 14 introns harboring LAGLIDADG and GIY-YIG homing endonucleases.

Comparative Mitogenomics (r = +0.96)

Across twelve Pleurotus mitogenomes, size varies widely from 60,694 bp to 102,950 bp. Uniform reannotation revealed that mitogenome expansion in the genus is driven almost entirely by intron and homing endonuclease gene acquisition:
